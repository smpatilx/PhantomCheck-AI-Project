Educational Toolkit

The SIFT Media-Literacy Method

Four simple habits to evaluate questionable online claims before believing or reposting

S

Stop

Pause when you feel strong emotional reactions (outrage, fear, panic, greed). Disinformation thrives on emotional impulsivity.

I

Investigate Source

Check who authored the message or registered the domain. Look for an official 'About' page or press office verification.

F

Find Better Coverage

Search established news agencies or independent fact-checkers (e.g. Reuters Fact Check, Snopes, AP News) for the same claim.

T

Trace Claims

Follow quotations, charts, or video snippets back to their original unedited context before assuming the conclusion is valid.
